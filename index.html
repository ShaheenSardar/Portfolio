Experimental Evaluation of EC2-Based and Serverless Architectures for Bursty Web Application Workloads on AWS: Performance, Scalability, and Cost Trade-offs

Shaheen Sardar
Zenodo Preprint, 2026

I compared an EC2 t3.small server with API Gateway plus Lambda on identical CPU-bound logic across 36 trials and 6 workloads. Under a sudden burst, serverless cut average latency by 32.1% and raised throughput by 14.5%, but failed 0.434% of requests (EC2: 0%) and cost 9.5x more per 1,000 successful requests. Under sustained load, serverless P95 latency rose 57.2%, so the best architecture depends on the shape of the workload.
